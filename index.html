Our Story

A Taste of Royalty Since 1998

Nestled in the heart of the city, The Royal Plate was born from a simple dream — to serve food that feels like a celebration. For over two decades, our chefs have blended timeless recipes with modern artistry, sourcing the freshest local ingredients to craft dishes that delight every sense.

From intimate dinners to grand gatherings, our warm ambience and attentive service make every visit unforgettable. We don't just serve meals — we curate memories on a plate.
